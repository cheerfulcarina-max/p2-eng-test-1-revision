🍎 Chapter 2: Yummy Fruit & Plural Nouns

Remember: singular (1) vs plural (+s, +es, y ➔ ies)!

Grammar Tip Included
Rule 1: Add -s

a banana ➔ bananas
an apple ➔ apples

Rule 2: Add -es (-ch/-sh)

a peach ➔ peaches
a bench ➔ benches

Rule 3: y ➔ -ies

a cherry ➔ cherries
a strawberry ➔ strawberries
